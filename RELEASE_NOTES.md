# Glyph Arena 0.1.1

The family's look, carried properly: Arena is not SwiftUI, so it has to hold the Glyph tokens itself.

- **One typeface.** The system sans everywhere, headings in the same face as the body. The serif display face is gone.
- **One accent.** Office green and grey, nothing else: no cyan pills or bars, no purple tiles. A model's mark is its initial in grey, and a model that runs on your own machine wears the green house. Amber and red stay for warnings and errors.
- **Fewer words.** No paragraph under a heading, no caption under a number, and a route's reason opens when you tap the row instead of repeating itself down the page. A recipe says only what is not the plain default.
- **The sidebar** is the icon, the name and the gear, on one line.
- A duel's own clock, a busy machine and a folder that is not a vault are all handled honestly now (engine 1.10.11 to 1.10.14).

Apple Silicon, macOS 13 or later. Signed and notarized.
